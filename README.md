# Bio Emotion Tracker

A comprehensive health monitoring system that uses brain wave sensors and body parameter tracking to analyze emotional patterns and provide personalized recommendations for optimal health and happiness.

## Concept

Modern health tracking focuses primarily on physical metrics (steps, heart rate, sleep). However, the connection between our emotions, brain activity, and overall health is profound but poorly understood.

**Bio Emotion Tracker** proposes a 30-day continuous monitoring system that:
- Measures brain waves (EEG) and body parameters in real-time
- Correlates physiological changes with emotional states
- Identifies patterns between emotions, activities, and health outcomes
- Generates personalized recommendations for optimal well-being
- Provides a comprehensive summary of what makes you healthiest and happiest

## Vision

Enable individuals to:
- Understand their emotional-physiological connection
- Identify activities that promote health and happiness
- Recognize early warning signs of stress or imbalance
- Make data-driven decisions about lifestyle
- Optimize daily routines for maximum well-being

## Key Features

### Multi-Modal Sensing
- **EEG (Electroencephalography)**: Monitor brain wave patterns (alpha, beta, gamma, delta, theta)
- **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)**: Measure autonomic nervous system balance
- **Galvanic Skin Response (GSR)**: Track stress and emotional arousal
- **Body Temperature**: Monitor physiological state
- **Blood Oxygen (SpO2)**: Assess cardiovascular health
- **Motion/Activity**: Track physical movement and posture
- **Respiration Rate**: Monitor breathing patterns

### Emotional State Detection
- Real-time emotion classification (calm, stressed, happy, anxious, focused, etc.)
- Pattern recognition for emotional triggers
- Circadian rhythm analysis
- Sleep quality assessment
- Stress level monitoring

### Pattern Analysis
- Correlation between activities and emotional states
- Identification of optimal times for different activities
- Detection of environmental triggers
- Social interaction impact analysis
- Work-life balance assessment

### Personalized Recommendations
- Optimal daily schedule based on your biology
- Activity suggestions for specific emotional states
- Stress reduction techniques personalized to your patterns
- Sleep optimization recommendations
- Diet and exercise timing suggestions

### 30-Day Comprehensive Summary
- Emotional baseline establishment
- Identification of peak performance windows
- Health and happiness optimization strategies
- Long-term trend analysis
- Actionable insights for sustainable well-being

## Scientific Foundation

### Brain Waves and Emotions
- **Alpha waves (8-12 Hz)**: Relaxed, calm state
- **Beta waves (13-30 Hz)**: Active, focused, alert
- **Gamma waves (30-100 Hz)**: High-level processing, peak performance
- **Delta waves (0.5-4 Hz)**: Deep sleep, healing
- **Theta waves (4-8 Hz)**: Creativity, meditation, drowsiness

### Body Parameters and Emotions
- **HRV**: High HRV indicates relaxed state, low HRV indicates stress
- **GSR**: Increases with emotional arousal and stress
- **Temperature**: Changes with emotional state and activity
- **Respiration**: Fast/shallow with stress, slow/deep with relaxation

### Evidence-Based Insights
- Circadian rhythms affect emotional regulation
- Physical activity improves mood and cognitive function
- Sleep quality impacts emotional stability
- Social interactions influence physiological state
- Environmental factors (light, sound, temperature) affect well-being
